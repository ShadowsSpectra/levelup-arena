# LevelUP Arena — Product Roadmap

## Product direction
LevelUP Arena develops from an AI-powered negotiation training experience into a platform for practicing and assessing professional communication skills. The long-term focus includes corporate learning (B2B): training teams on realistic workplace situations, adapting learning content to business needs, and supporting repeatable practice.

This roadmap describes proposed product directions, not features currently available. Implementation priorities and scope are subject to validation.

## Workshop — content creation
**Status: concept.** A future workspace for creating and organizing training content.

- **Opponent builder:** configure negotiation personas, communication styles, and difficulty for relevant workplace situations.
- **Scenario builder:** define negotiation context, participant goals, constraints, and desired outcomes.
- **Knowledge-check builder:** create short assessments linked to professional skills and training scenarios.
- **Team content library:** organize and reuse learning materials within a team or organization. Broader sharing may be explored later, subject to access and moderation requirements.

**Initial interface direction:** a browsable overview of these four capabilities with representative examples and clear future-feature labeling. No editing, publishing, or saving is implied by the concept interface.

## Marketplace — engagement and personalization
**Status: exploratory concept.** Optional cosmetic personalization and recognition, such as mascot appearances, profile themes, and achievement titles. These may support learner motivation but are not the core B2B value proposition.

The concept does not imply a working store, payment flow, purchasable items, in-game currency, or entitlement system. XP remains a measure of progress, not a spending currency.

## B2B development priorities
- Training scenarios aligned with team roles and workplace needs.
- Content management and access boundaries appropriate for organizations.
- Meaningful learning outcomes and reporting, subject to separate product and technical design.

## Delivery boundaries
Current application behavior and existing training/negotiation flows remain unchanged. Workshop and Marketplace concept screens, if introduced, must be visibly distinguished from implemented functionality. Editors, user-generated content storage, organizational administration, analytics, commerce, and related APIs require separate scoping and approval.

## Status definitions
- **Implemented:** available and functional in the product.
- **Concept:** a proposed user experience or interface direction; not yet functional.
- **Planned:** accepted for a future delivery phase, subject to prioritization.
